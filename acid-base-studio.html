Definitions and assumptions

The experiments respond immediately. Readings begin hidden so you can make a prediction. Hide them again whenever you change the quantity. The practice view does not display these readings.

Use a calculator and show your reasoning. Enter concentrations in mol dm⁻³, amounts in mol, volumes in cm³ and pH to two decimal places, as labelled. Scientific notation such as 5e-3 or 5 × 10⁻³ is accepted.

Numbers can be checked here. Written explanations and equations are for discussion with your tutor; they are not automatically marked. Your work stays in this tab when you change experiments, but reloading clears it. Nothing is sent or saved online.
